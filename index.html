Addressing Heat-Related Health Risks

Dehydration Risk

Extreme temperatures in regions like the UAE significantly increase dehydration risks, leading to severe health consequences if not addressed promptly.

Late Detection

Delayed recognition of symptoms often leads to emergency medical cases, resulting in higher morbidity and mortality rates among vulnerable populations.

Genetic Variability

Each individual's susceptibility to heat stress varies based on their unique genetic makeup, making it difficult to predict and prevent effectively.

How The Gene Patch Works

The Science of Heat Response

Heat Stress

Genes alter physiological adaptations that protect against dehydration and heat-related illness.

Water Balance

Genetics play a crucial role in maintaining proper hydration levels and electrolyte balance under heat stress.

Oxidative Stress

Genetics impact resilience to cellular stress caused by extreme heat exposure, affecting overall heat tolerance.

Laboratory Medicine

Biomarker screening identifies genetic indicators to predict individual responses to heat. We utilize non-invasive methods (saliva and sweat analysis) for early prediction, empowering timely healthcare interventions without the need for blood draws.

Responsible Innovation

Ethics & Data Privacy

  • No Real Data: The project does not utilize any real genetic data; all data is simulated.
  • No Storage: Participant privacy is strictly maintained with no personal data storage.
  • Educational Purpose: The Gene Patch serves as an educational prototype to demonstrate concepts safely and ethically.

Sustainability Impact

  • Emergency Reduction: Early detection reduces emergency cases and minimizes strain on healthcare resources.
  • Hospital Burden: Proactive measures help decrease hospital admissions, allowing for better resource allocation.
  • Preventive Approach: Shifts focus toward long-term preventive medicine, fostering a healthier population and reducing costs.

Limitations & Future Vision

Current Limitation: The Gene Patch remains a conceptual prototype requiring further clinical validation.

Future Applications: Seamless integration with existing smart wearables, AI-driven personalized medicine, and climate-based health protection systems.
